# Revenue

Acme Corp reported total revenue of $4.2 billion in 2023, an increase of 12% over the prior year. Subscription revenue contributed $3.1 billion, while services revenue contributed $1.1 billion.

# Risk Factors

The principal risks include supply chain disruption, foreign exchange volatility, and increased competition in the enterprise segment. A prolonged outage of the primary cloud region could materially affect operations.

# Outlook

Management expects revenue between $4.8 billion and $5.0 billion in 2024, driven by international expansion and renewals.
