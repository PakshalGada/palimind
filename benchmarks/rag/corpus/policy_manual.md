# Data Retention

Audit logs are retained for seven years. Customer records are retained for the duration of the contract plus ninety days. Backup tapes are destroyed after eighteen months.

# Access Control

Access follows the principle of least privilege. Production access requires multi-factor authentication and approval from the security team. Quarterly access reviews are mandatory.

# Incident Response

A severity-one incident must be reported within fifteen minutes. The incident commander coordinates containment, communications, and the post-incident review.
